**V_tune 0.40 V vs the D20 monitor / calibration model:** this run uses a **fixed** band code (125) for the integer-N interface check, not the nearest-code calibrator. With D20, one band step is 0.24–0.29 V of V_tune, so a fixed code can land anywhere in a ~0.25 V slice of the 0.25–0.95 V window. The behavioural model with nearest-code cal lands in **0.34–0.82 V** (≥ 0.09 V inside the window edges). Margin from 0.40 V to the planned low monitor threshold (≈ 0.30 V) is **0.10 V** — about 0.3–0.4 of a band step — so a fixed-code test point can sit near the flag without meaning calibrated channels do. The V_tune monitor design should keep the low threshold at ≈ 0.30 V (below the calibrated floor 0.34 V) and treat 0.40 V as a band-dependent fixed-code case, not a calibration failure.

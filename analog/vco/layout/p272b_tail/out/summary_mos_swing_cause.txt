## Cause [SCH/HAND]

- **Rejected:** pure DC Ig×1 MΩ → lower Vgs → lower Itail. OP shows Itail
  change −0.1 %, not −22 %. The OTA loop holds nbf against the ~42 nA Ig.
- **Retained:** thin-ox moscap adds gate leakage *and* C(V) / channel
  conductance under the large-signal bias filter. Evidence: bias Sid +3.2 dB
  @ 100 kHz, VCO ΔL +0.516 dB, amp 1.20 → 0.93 V, f0 3.649 → 3.662 GHz with
  moscap vs ideal [SCH summary_d9_finding]. Mechanism is large-signal /
  noise up-conversion through the nonlinear leaky moscap, not a static
  Itail shift.
- Ideal / MIM have negligible Ig and flat C; rfcmim keeps the D9 filter
  (accepted path).

Design unchanged: MOS rejected (decisions 2026-11-13 D9).

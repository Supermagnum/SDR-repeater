## Flow lessons (apply to later blocks)

- Schematic must use **M/C/R devices**, not `X` subcircuit wrappers — else netgen builds placeholder cells and pin-permute fails.
- Metal1 pin polygons = **8/2**; labels = **8/25** (not boxes on 25).
- Gate Cont must sit on a **poly side-extension** outside Activ; Cont on the top overhang shorts G↔S/D on L=0.13 and trips Cnt.b.
- Gate M1 landing width ≤ 0.16 µm for L=0.13 (S/D gap ≈ 0.35 µm).
- NW tap (nactiv∩nwell + Cont + M1) ties PMOS bulk to vdd with `--disable_tap_extraction`.
- `cmim` PCell: pass `C` together with `w`/`l` and `Calculate=w&l` or size sticks at 6.99 µm.

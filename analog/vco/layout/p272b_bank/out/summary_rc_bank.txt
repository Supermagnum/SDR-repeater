## D24 carry (31→32) vs one LSB [SCH]

- Sum binary on-C/side = 12.38+23.79+46.98+93.00+185.56 = **361.71 fF**
- One thermo unit = **370.29 fF**
- Boundary step Δ = 370.29 − 361.71 = **8.58 fF** ≈ 0.69 × LSB (12.38)
- Flag threshold: |Δ − LSB| > ½ LSB → 8.58 vs 12.38, error 3.8 fF < 6.19 fF
  (½ LSB) → **no flag** (within ½ LSB of one LSB step).
- Monotonicity of raw MIM sizes: each wt doubles within trim; no non-monotonic
  raw c_mim sequence [SCH].

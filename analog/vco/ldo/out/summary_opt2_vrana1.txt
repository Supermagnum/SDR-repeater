# Study notes — option (2) through VR_ANA1 (STOP)

## Why VR_ANA1 was ~1.5 V
- LDO deck used Vin=1.5 V as the *input* to the VCO sub-LDO (dropout to 1.1 V), not as the LO/CP rail.
- LO chain and charge pump are designed for ~1.2 V (corners 1.08–1.32 V). VCO centre-tap ~1.1 V.
- No accepted block requires 1.5 V on VR_ANA1 itself. RX LNA headroom is Phase 3 TBD (often ≥1.2–1.8 V).

## Blocks vs voltage
| Block | Designed V | Can sit on filtered 1.1–1.2 V VR_ANA1? |
|---|---|---|
| VCO | ~1.1 V | Yes (D10) |
| LO CML/TSPC | ~1.2 V | Yes (already sim'd at 1.08–1.32) |
| Charge pump / PFD | ~1.2 V | Yes; ss already weak at 1.08 V / V_tune=0.95 — keep pad ≥~1.15 V after IR |
| RX LNA/TIA/ADC | TBD Phase 3 | Prefer **off** this pin (PSRR / headroom) → VR_ANA2 or internal LDO |

## Proposed repartition (drop-in: pin 3 stays VR_ANA1, pins 12/14 stay n.c.)
VBAT → one LDO → R → **VR_ANA1 pad** → bond → board 100 nF
VCO + LO + CP fed from the pad (chip side).
RX → VR_ANA2 (pin 25) with TX/DAC, or own internal LDO + on-chip decoupling.
Do not fight a second regulator on pin 3.

## IR @ ~10 mA
5 Ω × 10 mA = 50 mV (matches owner). Prefer Kelvin sense at the pad so VCO/LO/CP see the target voltage; LDO out sits IR higher.

## Noise of RX's new supply
- On VR_ANA2 + 100 nF: LinHT half-duplex and repeater (separate RX/TX RFICs) never run TX DAC and RX together on one die → board node quiet in RX. Residual: VR_ANA2 regulator noise; LNA PSRR typically makes tens of nV/√Hz acceptable [HAND] until Phase 3 budgets it.
- Internal RX LDO: isolates from TX; needs own on-chip C (area) — same class of problem as A/B/D but RX PSRR is far looser than VCO K_push.

## rf-frontend.md rows (proposed wording, not applied)
- Pin 3 VR_ANA1: "LO/VCO/CP quiet rail ~1.1–1.2 V (on-chip series R to LDO; 100 nF)"
- Pin 25 VR_ANA2: "TX/DAC (+ RX if moved off VR_ANA1); 100 nF"
- §7 Regulators: note VR_ANA1 = LO domain, VR_ANA2 = TX (+RX)

## Push at divider frequencies (not just f_ref)
Board 100 nF is behind bond L: at 175 MHz–2 GHz |Z_pad| ≈ 1–90 Ω, not 1/ωC.
1 mA spikes → mV-level bounce → discrete FM spurs at f_div offsets (L ≈ −60 to −80 dBc for 1 mA) — **not** at duplex 600 kHz / 2 MHz.
Duplex-band white S_v still set by LDO→R→C (1 MHz |Z|≈0.01 Ω).
On-chip local bypass at LO/CML supplies still wise for GHz bounce (separate from this study).

## Explained differences vs `vco_sim_flat`

1. **Tail:** `vco_sim_flat` uses a **stand-alone step2 tail** (mt 160u/4u ng=32, mr 20u/4u, iref) for range TB. Hierarchical `vco_tail_dac` is the **D22 OTA DAC** (mt 480u/4u ng=96, full DAC). Different by design — flat TB ≠ chip tail.
2. **Buffer:** flat has stand-in CML buffer (mb1/mb2 + loads); not in `vco_top` (LO buffer is P2.7.4).
3. **Ampcal:** in hierarchy (`ampcal_det`); omitted from `vco_sim_flat` VCO-only path.
4. **Ideal sources:** flat has vvb/iref/ib; hierarchy exposes pins (code*, g*, hold, rep) for digital / TB.
5. **Bank / core / var / L:** same generators → same devices and sizes as flat VCO path (range.json D24).

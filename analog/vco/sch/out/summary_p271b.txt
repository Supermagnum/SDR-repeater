## Finding — band steps broken by bypass C

Re-sizing/trim with off-state bank bypass on the plates does not restore even carries. Layout must **not** start until steps are fixed.

**Proposal (pick one):**
1. Re-work `trim()` / more passes with bypass present; or size bank bypass smaller / relocate so off-C does not scale badly with bit weight.
2. Keep bypass off the RF plates (only across bias R far from tank) if start-up RC still meets 0.4 µs.
3. Accept larger steps only with a D20 window / landing revisit (not preferred — 1.06 V > 0.7 V window).

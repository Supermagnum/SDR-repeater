Start-up to 90 % of 0.991 V: **1.0 ns** `[SCH]` (kick present; NMOS similar order).

NMOS core drawn-width proxy: ≈ 256 µm² `[HAND]`

## 5. Conclusion (comparison only — NMOS baseline unchanged)

| Item | NMOS (baseline) | HBT (this run) |
|---|---|---|
| Supply | VR_ANA1 ≈ 1.2 V (D21) | same; paper 1.6 V swing needs ≈ 1.8–2.5 V |
| I_tail | ≤ 4 mA (D12) | 4.0 mA |
| Amp @ mid | 1.16 V `[SCH]` | 0.99 V `[SCH]` |
| L@1 MHz duplex | −122.6 / −119.3 `[SCH]` | HAND fair ≈ NMOS ±0.6 dB; PSS untrusted |
| Band top | covers 4.20 GHz | **4.10 GHz — miss** `[SCH]` |
| BVCEO | N/A (MOS oxide) | OK at Amp≈1 V; 1.6 V swing exceeds min 1.4 V |
| Area core | ~256 µm² | ~25 µm² `[HAND]` |

**Conclusion: keep the NMOS core (D1).** On D21/D12 the HBT does not deliver the paper ≈ 5 dB /
1.6 V win; fair Leeson is a wash; the top of the band is short; VACASK HBT PSS L is unusable.
A higher CT rail would reverse D21 — not proposed. Design unchanged.

## Summary

- Keep NMOS (D1). No finding to switch.
- HBT PSS L untrusted (~35 dB vs Leeson).
- HBT top f = 4.10 GHz < 4.20 GHz `[SCH]`.

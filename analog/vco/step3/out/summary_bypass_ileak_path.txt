## Path verdict

The **40 nA at ff / 125 °C is not junction leakage to substrate.** It is
**channel (D→S) subthreshold** current (|I_S|/|I_D| ≈ 1.00, |I_B|/|I_D| ≈ 0.000).
True drain/source–bulk junction current in the circuit-like off bias (D=S=1.2 V,
B=0) is only ~9 pA total.

## Plate DC shift through 2 MΩ [HAND]

- If the bound-bias channel current (40 nA) flowed through R_bias: ΔV = 80 mV.
  That bias (V_DS=1.2 V) is **not** the stand-alone off-bit DC (V_DS≈0); it is the
  pessimistic extract used for the noise bound.
- Circuit-like substrate junction on one plate: ΔV ≈ 0.01 mV (negligible).

## Off switches under tank swing (ff hot, top band)

of plate voltage / IR drop. An LV NMOS cannot turn on. Plate swing only changes

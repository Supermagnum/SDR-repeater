## Monte Carlo, 3 samples per case [SCH]

Mismatch on every LO transistor (V_T, beta, W, L; PDK values), top frequency of each band (largest error in degrees). Phase error of each output against its ideal position, relative to the first output; duty cycle per output. UHF image rejection from the I/Q quadrature error alone (gain matched): IRR = 10 log10((1 + cos e) / (1 - cos e)), at the 3-sigma error.

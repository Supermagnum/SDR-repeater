# Provisional tank Q

Decision of 2026-10-17. Source: [EM], `reports/phase0-review/em-l2n0.md`.

Until the matched-mesh comparison required before Phase 2 is finished, tank calculations use the lower of the two solver results:

- 11.8 at 2.4 GHz
- 14.9 at 3.5 GHz

Those are the openEMS figures. Every report, schematic note, and phase-noise estimate that uses them is labelled provisional. The Phase 1 digital back end does not use this Q.
